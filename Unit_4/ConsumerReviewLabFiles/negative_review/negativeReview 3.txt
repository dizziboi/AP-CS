I don't write reviews. But this was sooooo bad. I didn't make it through the first 10 minutes (and yes, I didn't give it a chance). I like movies like Bad Santa. It's funny, irreverent, good writing, directing, and acting. So I thought I'd give Dear Santa a watch. The premise was good, but the execution was...terrible. The acting is sub par. The writing mediocre at best. The "jokes" seemed forced. I couldn't force myself to sit there until JB showed up. South Park's Christmas episodes are Christmas comedy gold compared to this. Which is what I think I'll watch next.
